VIP Café Reservation Leaflet

Client: Aux Merveilleux de Fred (Tokyo, Japan)

Graphic Design & Illustration: Alena Kandazhenka

2026

After the client introduced a new VIP reservation service in their café, they added a sheet of paper with the service description on the customer’s table. However, it was difficult to read, which resulted in misunderstandings. I rearranged the information and created a leaflet which made the service clear to the customer, saved the staff time and improved the customer experience.
