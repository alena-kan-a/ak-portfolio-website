Illustrations for Matcha Sweets Packaging

Client: Aux Merveilleux de Fred & The Matcha Tokyo

Packaging Design: AMFK

Product photography: Akiho

Vector illustrations of sweets created for black and white retail packaging. While on budget, the boxes are distinguishable and cost-efficient in comparison to coloured packaging. Illustrations were requiested to be in vector for potential further re-use (ex. for café menus).
