Social Media Graphic for Theatre

Client: Tokyo International Players

Graphic Design: Alena Kandazhenka

2025

Social media graphic I designed for Tokyo International Players, a non-profit theatre organization in Tokyo, Japan. The graphic was used on the organization’s social media channels and helped the client find talent who would volunteer their skills. The graphic had to align with previously posted content on the same topic, yet I was welcome to explore different visual interpretations.
