About

My name is Alena Kandazhenka, I am a graphic designer and illustrator currently based in Tokyo, Japan.

I am a professional fluent in English and Japanese, which allows me to create designs targeting a wider range of audiences. When creating a layout, I always keep the target audience in mind, and choose the most effective design for it.

In addition to graphic design, I have been actively utilizing my illustration skills, and I am applying them in my designs where appropriate.

In my work, I have a strong policy against the use of generative AI. I value my clients’ trust, and they can be sure that no potential copyright issues will arise as I either create assets for layouts myself or outsource them to other creative professionals.

I am open to different types of projects, and I am especially interested in printed materials and branding. I am looking forward to new opportunities and connections.
